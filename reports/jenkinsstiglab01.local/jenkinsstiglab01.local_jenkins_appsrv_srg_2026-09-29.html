JNKS-002 Open CAT I Jenkins authorization must enforce least privilege and deny anonymous access

SRG requirements

SRG-APP-000033, SRG-APP-000340

Result

Authorization strategy 'Logged-in users can do anything' makes every authenticated user an administrator.

Evidence

config.xml authorizationStrategy class=hudson.security.FullControlOnceLoggedInAuthorizationStrategy
Unauthenticated GET http://127.0.0.1:8080/api/json -> HTTP 403

Remediation

Use Matrix-based or Role-based authorization. Grant no permissions to 'anonymous', do not grant Overall/Administer to 'authenticated', and restrict Administer to named admins/groups. Do not use 'Anyone can do anything', 'Legacy mode', or 'Logged-in users can do anything'.
